Privacy Policy — Cosmos Publisher

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Cosmos Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it manages, to upload videos, set thumbnails and read statistics on those channels.

  • Data accessed: only the owner's own YouTube channels, through the official YouTube Data API, with the owner's explicit consent.
  • Use: uploading the owner's videos and thumbnails, and reading public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) for internal reports.
  • Storage: the access token is stored encrypted and is never shared. No data from other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.
  • Third parties: none. The tool is not offered to the public.
  • Revoking access: at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: crovnwav@gmail.com

Cosmos Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
